Overall score: equal-weight peer percentiles for FDR, MTTR, CSAT, SLO, reassurance, escalation rate, reopen rate, and missed chats per recorded week. Higher is better for FDR, CSAT, SLO and reassurance; lower is better for the others. Ticket volumes and backlog do not affect the score. All eight measures are required; incomplete engineers appear last. Ties share ranks. Scores are relative to the complete engineers in this quarter, not goal attainment. Counts are quarter totals; backlog is each engineer’s latest week. Existing QTD rate weighting applies.
